LumiTale Studio

Calm, five-minute bedtime stories for toddlers (ages 2–5), published on our YouTube channel LumiTale Bedtime.

LumiTale Autopilot

LumiTale Autopilot is our internal production tool. It writes and narrates each story, assembles the video, and – after a person has watched and approved it – uploads and schedules it on our own YouTube channel using the YouTube API Services. It also reads our channel's own statistics to plan new episodes. It is used only by the channel owner and is not available to the public.
